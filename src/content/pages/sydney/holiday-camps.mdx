# **ADVENTURES & TRAILS**

## [**TRAILS, ADVENTURES & GAMES**](https://www.trybooking.com/DPGFR)**&#x20;::** 

**VENUE: Davidson Park, Forestville**

**When:** Thursday 8 October 2026  **Camp Time:** 8.30am-1pm  **Cost:** $70 (includes Morning Tea, Water & Snacks)&#x20;

**What to bring:** Hat, hydration pack or soft water bottle (please ensure sunscreen applied before arrival)

Discover the thrill of Trail Running 🏃‍♂️and Orienteering 🧭 at our Half Day Trails, Adventures & Games Camp!

Why this camp?

✔️Ignite your child’s passion for nature and the great outdoors

✔️Explore our beautiful local trails on the Northern Beaches

✔️Learn the skill of orienteering

✔️Fun and Friendship

✔️Team Building & Resilience

**Program Outline:**

This 4.5 hour camp is for the outdoor enthusiasts and adventurers! We start with a run exploring the many windy single trails and fire trails we are spoiled with on our Northern Beaches (approx 4km) as we work our way towards our base camp for the morning. Here we will enjoy some morning tea, some good old fashioned park games, and an orienteering challenge!  Armed with a map, we split into small teams and set on our way to find numbered checkpoints whilst trying to decipher the best route choice to get there! Who will be first back to base and crack the codeword for the day?! After some more food, fun and games, we hit the trails again (approx 1.5km) for the final part of our adventure to our pick up point to conclude the camp for the day. 

*Children attending this camp must be comfortable continuously running/jogging 4-5km (ages 9-16)*

*Minimum group size: 8*
