# MIDDLE DISTANCE Pop-Up Clinics

Ready to take your 800m or 1500m racing to the next level?

Our next **Star-Athletics Middle Distance Clinic series** is designed for young athletes looking to develop the skills and confidence to race strongly over 800m and 1500m.

Whether your athlete is:\
🏅 Getting ready for **Zone, Regional or State competition**\
🏃‍♀️ Preparing for the upcoming **Little Athletics season**\
📈 Or simply looking to improve their middle-distance racing

These clinics will focus on the skills that can make a real difference on race day — including **pacing, race strategy, surging, speed development and finishing strongly**.

⭐ **What to expect:**\
• Event-specific training for 800m & 1500m\
• Race skills and tactical awareness\
• Developing speed to complement endurance\
• Learning how to manage different stages of a race\
• Practical strategies athletes can take straight into competition
