# ATHLETICS Pop-Up Clinics

Prepare for the upcoming athletics season with our specialist **Athletics Carnival Preparation Pop-Up Clinics**.

Whether your child is competing at their school athletics carnival, aiming to qualify for the next level, or preparing for zone, regional or state competition, our 75-minute clinics provide event-specific coaching to help athletes build confidence, refine their technique and perform at their best.

Led by experienced and accredited athletics coaches, athletes can choose to attend one clinic or multiple sessions to suit their goals.
