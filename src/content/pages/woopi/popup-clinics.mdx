**Seasonal Pop-Up Clinics for Young Athletes**

Looking to keep your child active and progressing throughout the year? Our **pop-up clinics** are designed to match the season – whether it's cross country season, athletics competitions, or off-season base training for team sports.

Led by Heather, each clinic focuses on building strength, speed, endurance, and technique in a supportive group setting. These sessions are a great way to stay sharp, build a fitness foundation, and have fun training with others.

🟢 **Cross Country** – Improve running form, pacing, and endurance

🟠 **Athletics** – Focus on sprints, jumps, and explosive power

🔵 **Team Fitness** – Build strength, speed, and aerobic fitness for footy, netball, soccer and more

**Clinics vary throughout the year** – keep an eye out for upcoming dates and locations!

**Pricing from $10 AUD per clinic.**
