**Run faster, move better and build confidence.**

Athletic Allstars is designed for primary school athletes who want to develop their **speed, running skills and overall athleticism** in a fun, supportive environment.

Sessions use games, drills and challenges to develop **running technique, speed, agility, coordination and endurance**, while building confidence and a positive attitude towards training and competition.

Athletes develop skills that support **athletics, cross country and other sports**, with activities adapted to suit each athlete's age, experience and stage of development.
