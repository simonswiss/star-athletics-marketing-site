### **Run faster, move smarter and compete with confidence.**

Track Squad helps athletes aged 10+ develop the speed, running technique, movement skills and confidence to perform across sprint, middle-distance and cross-country events.

**Event-specific training includes:**

* **Sprints:** stride mechanics, acceleration, maximum speed & speed endurance
* **Middle Distance & Cross Country:** race craft, pacing, speed & endurance

Athletes train together as **one team**, with sessions tailored to their event and ability.

**Suitable for:** Athletes aged 10+ developing their athletics and running performance.
