### Run faster, move smarter and compete with confidence

**Track Squad** brings sprinters and middle-distance/cross-country runners together as **one team**, with training tailored to the demands of their event.

Every session begins with a **shared warm-up**, developing movement skills, coordination, running technique and athletic foundations together. Athletes then split into event-specific groups for the main training set before coming back together for a **fun team finisher**.

### Sprints:

Develop the technical and physical qualities needed to run faster and more efficiently, with a focus on:

* **Stride mechanics** and running technique
* **Acceleration** and explosive starts
* **Maximum speed** and efficient sprinting
* **Speed endurance** and maintaining speed under fatigue

### Middle Distance & Cross Country:

Develop the skills and fitness needed to race strongly and intelligently, with a focus on:

* **Race craft** and tactical awareness
* **Pacing awareness** and learning to judge effort
* **Speed development** to improve running performance and finishing ability
* **Endurance** for sustained performance

### One Team. Different Goals. Shared Success.

Sessions are structured to challenge athletes appropriately for their event and level, while maintaining a **positive, supportive and engaging squad environment**.

Athletes are encouraged to challenge themselves, support their teammates, track their progress and enjoy the process of improvement.

**Suitable for:** Athletes aged 10+ looking to develop their sprint, middle-distance or cross-country performance while building strong foundations in running technique, athletic movement and confidence.
