### Senior Athletic Allstars

**Build confidence, develop running skills and prepare for the next step.**

Senior Athletic Allstars is designed for athletes in Years 5–6 who are ready for greater challenge and more structured training.

Sessions focus on **speed, running technique, agility and athletic movement**, helping athletes develop the skills, fitness and confidence to prepare for **athletics and cross country competition**, while also building a strong foundation for team sports.

**Suitable for:** Typically Years 5–6 (ages 10–12).
