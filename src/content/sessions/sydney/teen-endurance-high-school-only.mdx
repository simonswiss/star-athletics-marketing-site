**Build endurance, race with confidence and become a stronger runner.**

Teen Endurance is designed for high school athletes who enjoy distance running and want to improve their performance in **athletics, cross country and fun run/endurance events**.

Sessions develop **aerobic fitness, running efficiency, pacing, speed endurance and race skills**, while building confidence and resilience in a supportive squad environment.

Athletes train alongside like-minded runners and are encouraged to challenge themselves, work towards their goals and enjoy their running.

**Suitable for:** High school athletes with an interest in distance running and endurance events.
