**Build athletic skills, confidence and a love of movement.**

Athletic Allstars is a fun and engaging session where young athletes develop the **fundamental movement skills, coordination and athletic ability** needed for athletics and sport.

Sessions are adapted to suit different ages and abilities.

Athletes build **speed, agility, coordination, confidence and teamwork** while developing strong foundations for **athletics, cross country and other sports**.

**Suitable for:** Athletes aged 6–12 years.
