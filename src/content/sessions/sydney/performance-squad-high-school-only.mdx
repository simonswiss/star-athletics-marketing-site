### Performance Squad

**Structured training for athletes ready to take their performance further.**

Performance Squad is designed for motivated high school athletes who want to develop their **speed, endurance and overall athletic performance** for athletics, cross country and other sports.

Athletes train as **one squad**, beginning with a shared warm-up before splitting into **Speed and Endurance** groups for event-specific training.

With more challenging training and greater expectations, athletes are encouraged to take ownership of their development, work towards their goals and train alongside a motivated and supportive squad.

**Suitable for:** High school athletes aged 13+.
