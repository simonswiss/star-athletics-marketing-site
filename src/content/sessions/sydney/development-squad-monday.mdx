### Development Squad

**Take your athletic performance to the next level.**

Development Squad is designed for athletes who are ready for **more structured and specialised training**. Sessions build on the foundations developed in our younger squads, with a focus on improving speed, endurance, strength, running efficiency and event-specific performance.

Athletes develop the skills and confidence needed to progress in **athletics and cross country**, with training tailored to their event, ability and goals.

**Suitable for:** Typically ages 10+ and athletes ready for a more focused approach to their athletic development.
