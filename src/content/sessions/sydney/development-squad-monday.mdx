### **Take your athletic performance to the next level.**

Development Squad is designed for athletes who are ready for **more structured and specialised training**. Athletes train as one squad, with sessions divided into **Sprints and Middle Distance** groups so training can be tailored to the demands of each event.

**Sprints** focus on developing speed, acceleration, running mechanics, strength and speed endurance.

**Middle Distance** focuses on developing endurance, speed, pacing, race skills and event-specific fitness.

Both groups build the skills, fitness and confidence needed to progress in **athletics and cross country**, with training adapted to each athlete's ability and goals.

**Suitable for:** Typically ages 10+ and athletes ready for a more focused approach to their athletic development.
